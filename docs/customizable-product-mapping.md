## Method

The active Etsy listings in the made-to-order portion of DesiWeddingStudioUS were compared with the live LuxeMia Shopify catalog. A product was included only when the Etsy listing and LuxeMia product had the same primary product photo, and the LuxeMia variant had a DWS SKU. The matching image ID below is the numeric Etsy CDN image identifier found in both records.

The verified offer is intentionally narrow: custom color (confirmed by the shop owner for qualifying designs) and made-to-order construction from customer measurements. The qualifying Etsy sources carry an approximate 4–5 week total order window; LuxeMia must confirm the current production time and carrier transit separately after the color, measurements, fabric availability, and delivery address are known. No neckline, sleeve, padding, bottom-style, rush, or other design option is claimed unless separately confirmed in writing.

## Storefront safeguards

- Membership is handle-allowlisted; tags and title keywords cannot add unrelated products.
- Live Shopify controls price, availability, images, and the purchasable variant.
- The storefront overrides stale “ready to ship” display copy only for these verified handles; Shopify admin data is not mutated.
- Quick add and quick view are disabled for these products so shoppers see the color, measurement, production, shipping, and final-sale disclosures before adding an item.
