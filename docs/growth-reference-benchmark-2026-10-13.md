## Application

- Explain unstitched, semi-stitched, standard-size stitching and made-to-measure separately. Product-specific construction takes precedence over a category label.
- Keep LuxeMia's existing USD 10 alteration scope separate from full lehenga/choli construction.
- Competitor-derived planning range: USD 34–40 is Utsav's published lehenga stitching range; Cbazaar's sampled options are lower. This is a benchmark, not a recommended margin-tested LuxeMia fee.
- Preserve the owner's confirmed 3–4-business-day dispatch for stocked non-custom items. Do not add a competitor's processing time to LuxeMia checkout as an operational promise.
- Before any full-stitching sale, confirm design eligibility, measurements, total charge and extra processing time. Do not imply a full stitching option exists merely because competitors offer it.
- Promote the five reviewed ready-to-wear palazzo suits at their verified base prices (three Simar Silk colors at USD 77.50; Green and Rani Pink Chinon at USD 79.99). Exact-match competitive price advantage is unverified.
