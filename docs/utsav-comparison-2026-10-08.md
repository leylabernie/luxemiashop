## Price and delivery caveats

Prices above are displayed garment prices, not completed checkout totals. Utsav currently advertises “up to 20% off” and free shipping on carts US$100+, with duty-free USA delivery. The exact discount for these products was not verified at checkout. A hypothetical 20% discount on $133 gives $106.40, so it would be misleading to advertise a guaranteed 24.82% delivered saving.

LuxeMia's confirmed U.S. standard shipping is $14.99 below $150; a single $99.99 item therefore totals $114.98 before discounts and taxes. At two $99.99 items, the $199.98 subtotal qualifies for free U.S. standard shipping. LuxeMia's actual express checkout rate remains unresolved; do not copy Utsav's shipping or promise its arrival date.
